About Zenith Solutions, LLC

A growing Liberian-owned professional services firm.

Zenith Solutions, LLC is a legally registered, Liberian-owned multidisciplinary professional services firm based in Congo Town, Monrovia. The firm is structured to serve clients across commercial, financial, technical and institutional needs.

Its team includes experienced tax practitioners, accountants, engineers, trade finance professionals and other specialists equipped to provide practical and relevant business solutions.
